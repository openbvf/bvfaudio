BvfAudio is a private app to record and play back audio on macOS. Audio is encrypted as it's captured and decrypted only inside the app where you play it back, so there's never a readable copy on disk for Spotlight, backups, other software, or people using your computer to find.

iOS is record-only by design: the private key never leaves your Mac, so iPhone and iPad can record new audio but can never play it back.

On-device transcription (no cloud); transcribed text can be saved encrypted to your Bedit journal if you have one. Browse by date and filter by tag. Export selection. Idle auto-lock.

There is no account, no server, no backend. No analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting. BvfAudio makes no network calls of its own.

Open-source, no lock-in: every recording is a file named by date, decryptable from the command line with bvf-cli (Homebrew installable); decrypted contents are standard AAC. Full docs and source on GitHub.
